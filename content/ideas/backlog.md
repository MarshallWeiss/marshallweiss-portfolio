- **How to Save Money on AI Tools: The Strategic Free-Tier Playbook** - Practical guide to minimizing AI costs without sacrificing capability. Token economics explained (why ChatGPT costs what it costs, what you're actually paying for), cycling through free tiers strategically (Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity all have daily limits), workflow optimization to reduce token usage (when to use which model, Haiku vs Sonnet vs Opus), understanding pricing models (per-token vs subscription vs usage-based), free alternatives that actually work (local models, open source options), and the hidden costs people miss (context window waste, inefficient prompting, using expensive models for simple tasks). Real tactics for designers/developers building with AI who don't have unlimited budgets. Not "be cheap" but "be strategic."

- **Build Workflows as Agents, Not Conversations** - Why every experimental workflow in Claude should be built as an agent, not just done in chat. When you're figuring out a process (article writing, research, design workflow), building it as an agent means it gets documented, becomes reusable, can be iterated on, and can be shared. "Building in public" for the AI era - your experimentation becomes knowledge others can use. The article writing agent we built is an example: we discovered a 7-stage workflow by doing it, and now it's saved and shareable. Every time you experiment, ask: "Should this be an agent?"

- **The Copyright Fiction: Why Stealing Is Easier Than Ever (And the Law Pretends Not to Notice)** - How AI makes it trivially easy to "borrow" someone's style, voice, and approach while technically avoiding infringement. Upload Atlantic articles, train on their patterns, output new work that feels identical but legally distinct. Copyright law assumes copying requires effort and leaves obvious traces—but what happens when you can absorb and remix someone's life's work in an afternoon? The law is designed for photocopiers in a world of neural networks. Not just "copyright is outdated" but: what does ownership even mean when style, voice, and approach—the actual value—can be extracted and redeployed without technically copying a single sentence? And if we can't protect that, what exactly are we protecting?

- **Building Design Systems for AI Context** - How to document design systems so AI can actually understand and use them correctly (not just for humans)
- **Component Library Selection for AI Workflows** - Why your choice of base library (shadcn, MUI, Chakra, etc.) matters when working with AI tools
- **AI as a Handoff Filter** - Using AI to standardize and improve designer-to-developer communication, reducing misunderstandings and rework
